# Claims Manual Excerpt (EXAMPLE — not carrier authority)

## FNOL completeness
- Capture date/time of loss, location, parties, vehicles/property, injuries, police report number when available.
- Do not admit liability in intake notes.

## Coverage determination
- Tentative only until policy verification against the declarations and forms.
- Cite the form/endorsement name when stating an exclusion.

## Fraud indicators
- Indicators support SIU referral; they are not findings of fraud.
- Never accuse the insured or claimant of fraud in customer-facing text.

## Severity
- Minor: property-only, no injury, clear single vehicle/property damage under desk threshold.
- Moderate: multi-party or unclear liability, possible injury.
- Major: severe injury, fatality, commercial exposure, or CAT involvement.
