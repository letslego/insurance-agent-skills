# FNOL Intake

Capture a complete, usable first notice of loss. Separate **reported facts**, **customer statements**, and **open questions**. Do not invent coverage or liability conclusions here.

## Steps

1. Identify LOB, policy identifiers (if known), reporter role, loss date/time/location.
2. Capture what happened in chronological bullets; mark unknowns explicitly.
3. Collect vehicles/property involved, injuries, police/fire report numbers, photos/video available.
4. Note immediate needs: towing, rental, glass, emergency mitigation, medical.
5. Flag urgency (injury, total loss potential, catastrophe, fraud indicators → hand to specialist skills).
6. Produce FNOL summary + missing-info checklist for the next owner.

## Output shape

**FNOL summary** · **Parties/units** · **Injuries/property** · **Immediate needs** · **Missing facts** · **Suggested next skill** (`coverage-determination`, `liability-assessment`, `severity-triage`, `fraud-red-flags`)

**Done when:** another adjuster can open the claim without re-asking the basics already provided.
