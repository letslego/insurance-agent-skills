# Coverage Determination

Analyze coverage from **policy forms + facts in file**. Label conclusions as covered / not covered / partial / insufficient facts. This is decision support — cite form sections when available; never invent policy wording.

## Steps

1. Confirm policy period, named insured, covered auto/location, drivers, endorsements known.
2. Restate loss facts that matter to coverage (cause, date, who, where, permission, business use, etc.).
3. Walk insuring agreement → exclusions → exceptions → conditions → limits/deductibles/sublimits.
4. Identify grey areas needing more facts, recorded statement, or coverage counsel.
5. Recommend: accept, deny (with reasons), partial accept, or ROR / investigate further.

## Output shape

| Issue | Policy reference | Fact | Conclusion |
|-------|------------------|------|------------|

**Coverage posture** · **Open questions** · **Customer-safe explanation draft** (no legalese dump)

**Done when:** every material coverage question has a conclusion or a precise fact gap.
