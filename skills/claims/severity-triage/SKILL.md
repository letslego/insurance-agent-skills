# Severity Triage

Route the claim to the right handling track quickly.

## Steps

1. Summarize damage description, airbags, driveability, injuries, prior damage.
2. Classify severity band: glass/low · moderate repair · heavy · probable total · injury/complex.
3. Recommend inspections (photos, virtual, field, DRP) and rental/transport needs.
4. Flag special handling: injury, attorney, uninsured, rideshare, commercial use, CAT.
5. Suggest reserves posture qualitatively if facts support it (no fake precision).

## Output shape

**Severity band** · **Handling track** · **Inspection plan** · **Customer logistics** · **Watch items**

**Done when:** the claim has a clear next operational path within 24–48 hours.
