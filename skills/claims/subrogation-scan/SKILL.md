# Subrogation Opportunity Scan

Find recovery paths early.

## Steps

1. Identify potentially responsible third parties (other driver, municipality, manufacturer, contractor, landlord).
2. Check evidence needed: liability proof, damages docs, insurance IDs, spoliation risks.
3. Estimate recovery attractiveness qualitatively (liability clarity × damages × collectability).
4. Recommend: pursue / monitor / close — and statute/notice watch-outs if known.
5. Draft handoff note for subrogation team.

## Output shape

**Recovery theory** · **Evidence checklist** · **Attractiveness** · **Next actions** · **Handoff note**

**Done when:** subro either has a pursuit plan or a documented reason not to pursue.
