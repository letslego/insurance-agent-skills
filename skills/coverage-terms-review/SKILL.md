# Coverage Terms Review

Design or critique the **coverage skeleton**: what is granted, what is excluded, where attachment sits, and what gaps remain.

## Steps

1. Restate requested coverage (LOB, forms if named, limits, deductibles/SIRs, additional insureds, waivers, MSAs).
2. Check fit vs hazard picture: does the form match the ops (e.g., products, professional, cyber, inland marine)?
3. Stress gaps: sublimits, excluded causes, residential exclusions, absolute pollution, cross-suits, insured-vs-insured, prior acts, occurrence vs claims-made.
4. Propose modifications: endorsements to add/remove, higher deductibles, margin clauses, warranty subjectivities.
5. List **deal-breaker terms** vs **nice-to-have** broker asks.

## Output shape

| Element | Requested | Recommended | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-----------|

Then: **Gaps**, **Must-have endorsements**, **Subjectivities**, **Broker pushback expected**.

**Done when:** a coherent terms sheet exists and every material gap has a recommendation or an explicit accept risk.
