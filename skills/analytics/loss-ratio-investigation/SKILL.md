# Loss Ratio Investigation

Diagnose where and why results moved.

## Steps

1. Define period, LOB, and metric definitions from provided data.
2. Slice by segment (geo, channel, tenure, driver age, vehicle, peril) available in the data.
3. Separate frequency vs severity vs premium inadequacy.
4. Rank top contributors to the delta.
5. Propose analyses, UW actions, or pricing questions next.

## Output shape

**Headline** · **Bridge of change** · **Top segments** · **Freq/sev split** · **Next analyses / actions**

**Done when:** leadership knows the top 3 drivers and the next analytical move.
