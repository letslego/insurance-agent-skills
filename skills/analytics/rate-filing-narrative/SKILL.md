# Rate Filing Narrative

Turn exhibits into a coherent story regulators and internal stakeholders can follow. Do not invent numbers.

## Steps

1. Inventory exhibits provided (indication, trends, relativity, competitive, capping).
2. State the ask: overall rate change and key segment moves.
3. Explain drivers of indication in plain language.
4. Address consumer impact and mitigation (caps, transitions) if provided.
5. Draft executive summary + filing narrative section.

## Output shape

**Ask** · **Why now** · **Key exhibits cited** · **Consumer impact** · **Draft narrative**

**Done when:** a non-actuary can explain the filing without the spreadsheet open.
