# Handoff Brief

Make the next owner productive in under a minute.

## Steps

1. Identify from → to team and why transferring.
2. One-paragraph case abstract.
3. Decisions already made; decisions needed.
4. Attachments/systems pointers (claim #, policy #, docs).
5. Landmines (angry customer, attorney, DOI, fraud).
6. Requested action and due timing.

## Output shape

**Abstract** · **Done / Needed** · **Pointers** · **Landmines** · **Ask + due date**

**Done when:** the receiving team does not need to re-discover the thread.
