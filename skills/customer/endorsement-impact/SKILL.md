# Endorsement Impact

Show what changes operationally and financially when an endorsement is requested.

## Steps

1. Restate the requested change and effective timing.
2. List underwriting / eligibility checks triggered.
3. Premium direction and billing effects if data exists; else mark unknown.
4. Coverage consequences (who is excluded, waiting periods, inspection needs).
5. Documents needed and customer confirmation script.

## Output shape

**Change summary** · **Eligibility checks** · **Coverage effects** · **Premium/billing effects** · **Docs needed**

**Done when:** the customer knows what happens if the change is processed today.
