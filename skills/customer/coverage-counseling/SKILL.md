# Coverage Counseling

Help the customer choose knowingly. Present tradeoffs; do not hard-sell. Stay inside product rules provided.

## Steps

1. Learn household assets, risk tolerance, budget, must-have concerns (loan/lease, teen driver, home, pets).
2. Map current/proposed coverages vs common gaps (UM/UIM, collision, comprehensive, liability limits, medical, roadside).
3. Offer 2–3 structured options: minimum compliant · balanced · higher protection.
4. Call out state minimums vs recommended only when guidelines provided.
5. Confirm understanding with a teach-back summary.

## Output shape

**Needs snapshot** · **Option A/B/C table** · **Gaps called out** · **Recommended next step**

**Done when:** the customer can state what they chose and what they declined.
