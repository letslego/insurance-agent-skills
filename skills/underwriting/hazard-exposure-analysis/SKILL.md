# Hazard & Exposure Analysis

Build an exposure map the pricing and terms can hang on. Prefer schedule math and COPE/ops facts over adjectives.

## Steps

1. Define exposure bases (TIV by location/COPE, units, vehicles, payroll/class, revenue by ops, acreage, etc.).
2. For property: construction, occupancy, protection, exposure (COPE); roof/year; protection class; adjoining hazards.
3. For CAT: wind/quake/flood/wildfire zones if data exists; concentration / PML concerns; stacking.
4. For casualty/ops: products, premises, completed ops, auto, employers liability drivers; contractual risk transfer.
5. Rank top exposure concentrations and hazard drivers (max 7).
6. Map each top driver to a control, term, or price response.

## Output shape

- Exposure summary table
- Top hazard drivers
- Concentration / clash concerns
- Recommended surveys or subjectivities
- What would change the view (missing SOV fields, inspections, eng reports)

**Done when:** the largest loss drivers are named and each has a concrete UW response.
