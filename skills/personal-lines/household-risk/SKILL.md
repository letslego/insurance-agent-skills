# Household Risk

See the household as one risk system: drivers, vehicles, property, and shared exposures.

## Steps

1. Inventory people, vehicles, properties, and in-force policies.
2. Flag concentration risks (teen drivers, sports cars, coastal property, dogs/breeds if relevant and provided, business use).
3. Check multi-policy eligibility and bundling benefits per guidelines.
4. Identify cross-line implications (claim on home affecting package, excluded driver on auto).
5. Recommend package posture and gaps to close.

## Output shape

**Household map** · **Concentration risks** · **Bundle opportunity** · **Gaps** · **Recommended package actions**

**Done when:** the household story is coherent across lines.
