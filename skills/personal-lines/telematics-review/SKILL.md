# Telematics Review

Explain telematics outcomes without overclaiming model internals.

## Steps

1. Summarize available signals (braking, phone use, time of day, mileage, score band) from provided data only.
2. Separate score outcome vs behaviors the customer can change.
3. Map to UW/pricing action per guidelines (discount, neutral, surcharge, ineligible).
4. Draft coaching tips that are practical and non-shaming.
5. Note data gaps (short tenure, multiple drivers on device).

## Output shape

**Signal summary** · **Pricing/UW impact** · **Coaching tips** · **Caveats**

**Done when:** customer or UW knows what the data implies and what can change next term.
