# Regulatory Complaint Response

Build a complete, factual regulator-facing package. Tone: respectful, precise, non-argumentative.

## Steps

1. Restate the allegation in neutral terms.
2. Chronology of company actions with dates and owners.
3. Attach/list supporting exhibits (policy, letters, claim notes, recordings log).
4. Explain compliance with applicable process (only cite rules provided).
5. State outcome/remedy and preventive action if warranted.
6. Draft response letter + internal checklist of enclosures.

## Output shape

**Allegation** · **Chronology** · **Company position** · **Exhibits list** · **Draft letter**

**Done when:** a reviewer can send or escalate the draft with exhibits mapped.
