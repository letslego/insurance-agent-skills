# Interaction QA Scoring

Score an interaction against a rubric. Be specific with timestamps or quote snippets when available.

## Steps

1. Load the rubric dimensions provided (disclosure, empathy, accuracy, compliance, ownership).
2. Score each dimension with evidence.
3. Call out critical fails (misrepresentation, prohibited promises, privacy issues).
4. Give 2–3 coaching actions prioritized by risk.
5. Draft a short calibration note for QA teams.

## Output shape

**Scorecard** · **Critical fails** · **Coaching plan** · **Calibration notes**

**Done when:** another QA reviewer could reach a similar score from the same evidence.
