# Pricing Rationale

Tell a clear price story: exposure × hazard × experience × terms × marketplace. Show your work; label estimates vs rated outputs.

## Steps

1. State rating method available (manual rate, model output, competitor indication, judgment) and its limits.
2. Anchor exposure base and any ILFs / increased limits if relevant.
3. Apply experience and schedule mod logic from loss and hazard findings (credits/debits with reasons).
4. Adjust for terms (deductible relativity, coverage grants/restrictions).
5. Separate **technical indication**, **target quote**, and **walk-away**.
6. Note competitive context only if provided; do not invent market rates.

## Output shape

```
Technical indication: …
Target quote: …
Walk-away: …
Key debits: …
Key credits: …
Sensitivity: what ± changes price most
```

**Done when:** another underwriter can see why this price is not a round number pulled from air.
