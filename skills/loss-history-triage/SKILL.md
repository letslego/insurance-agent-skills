# Loss History Triage

Turn loss runs into an underwriting story: frequency, severity, large-loss drivers, and what must change for the risk to be acceptable.

## Steps

1. Inventory loss data: years, valuation date, ground-up vs net, caps, open/closed, policy type.
2. Compute (or estimate if partial) frequency and severity by year; call out incomplete years.
3. Isolate **large losses** and **shock losses**; capture cause, date, paid/reserved, status, remediation.
4. Tag patterns: same location, same cause code, seasonal spikes, liability vs property, work-comp body part/cause clusters.
5. Assess credibility: thin history, M&A, ops change, COVID-distorted years, missing prior carrier.
6. Translate to underwriting action: rate, deductible, exclusion, risk-control subjectivity, decline, or more info.

## Output shape

| Year | Count | Total incurred | Large losses | Notes |
|------|-------|----------------|--------------|-------|

Then: **Trend**, **Top causes**, **Open claim risk**, **UW implications**, **Questions for broker**.

**Done when:** the loss story can explain the last 3–5 years and each material implication has a recommended term or price response.
